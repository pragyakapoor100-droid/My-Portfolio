Client Audits

Two full client audits — Enfobar (e-commerce) and Home Detox UK (health & recovery) — every issue identified, diagnosed, and fixed.

Enfobar — E-Commerce SEO Audit
UK-based e-commerce brand · 7 critical issues identified & resolved · +195% traffic growth in period
E-Commerce
Issue 01 · Keywords
Wrong-country keyword ranking
US clicks doubled UK clicks despite targeting UK/Ireland. Identified low-difficulty, high-volume keywords (avg. KD 16.67%, vol. 31,200) to refocus geo-targeting toward intended markets.
Issue 02 · On-Page
Missing H1 headings across all product pages
Product and category pages lacked H1 tags — a fundamental indexation signal. Identified all affected URLs and delivered a full remediation checklist.
Issue 03 · Content
No descriptions + duplicate content
Hundreds of pages had blank descriptions. Colour-variant pages shared identical copy — triggering keyword cannibalization. Recommended consolidation, canonical tags, and product schema.
Issue 04 · UX
Missing product images sitewide
Key product pages lacked images — reducing SERP visibility and user trust. Identified all affected URLs and mapped an image optimisation protocol.
Issue 05 · Core Web Vitals
Mobile performance score: 36
LCP of 10.8s and TBT of 2,010ms on mobile vs LCP 1.4s on desktop. Delivered prioritised fix roadmap: image compression, JS reduction, blocking-resource elimination.
Issue 06 · Indexation
Canonical tag errors + 404 pages
Product variant pages weren't indexed due to canonical misconfigurations. 404 errors wasted crawl budget. Mapped full 301 redirect strategy and sitemap cleanup.
Issue 07 · Technical
Robots.txt blocking key trust pages
Shipping, Privacy Policy, and Terms of Service pages were blocked from crawling. Recommended directive removal to restore indexation of critical trust signals.
